# Interview Practice Gaps

Durable weaknesses from the three live interview-practice surfaces. This is one shared sink because
the same concept can fail in recall, HR framing, and code review; `Surface` keeps their consumers
separate. Timed build simulations keep their rubric-driven gaps in `practice/simulations/MISTAKES.md`.

## Open

| ID | Date | Surface | Level | Concept | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

IDs are monotonic `INT-NNNN` across both sections. Writers upsert by `Surface + Level + Concept` and
append new evidence instead of creating duplicates. Each practice prompt reads its own open rows before
choosing questions or focus.

## Closed

| ID | Closed | Surface | Level | Concept | Resolution evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

A demonstrated clean retry removes the row from Open and appends it here atomically. Closed IDs are
never reused.
